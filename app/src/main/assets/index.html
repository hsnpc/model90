How to Use

  • Start, stop, or latch. Use Start / Stop or press the winding key on the right. Press the top pendulum clip to latch or release the rod (stopping first if running).
  • Set the tempo. Slide the weight up to slow down or down to speed up. You can also use − / +, or press Tap Tempo in rhythm. Tap Tempo calculates BPM from the average interval between up to five recent taps.
  • Set the bell. Press Bell to switch between Classic and Slider. The bell rings on the first beat of each measure. Choose 0 to turn it off.
  • Use the bell lever. Tap the lever (left on Modern, upper-right on Classic) to cycle through the settings, or drag it outward for more beats per measure and inward for fewer.
  • Adjust in 1 BPM steps. Turn Scale Snap off to select any whole BPM. Turn it on to use the marked tempos.
  • Hide the controls. Use Hide UI to see just the metronome. Press Show UI to bring the controls back.
  • Adjust tempo (30–300 BPM). Sweep the OLED rotary dial (or drag horizontal digits on Retro LCD), use the tempo slider, − / +, or Tap Tempo.
  • Beats & accents. Tap or drag the beat number to choose 1–6 (or 1–12) beats, and tap individual beat circles on screen to cycle Accent → Normal → Mute.
  • Rhythm subdivisions. Switch subdivisions directly or open the quick-pick grid, and configure 4 / 6 / 9 rhythm sets in Settings.
  • OLED & Retro LCD consoles. Switch between modern OLED and 7-segment Retro LCD consoles in Settings, each with dedicated electronic timbres and palettes.
  • Keep unlocked & return. Press Keep Unlocked to keep Digital Mode available in App Info (ⓘ), or press Mechanical anytime to return to Model 90.
